## What you are building (3-6 bullets)
- **Pre-flight quote** (`POST /v1/broadcasts/:id/preflight`): audience count, per-reason skip counts, **client-level** already-messaged deferrals from `recipient_send_buckets`, billable × resolved `price_key` rate in integer paise, wallet before/after, tier/cap/sent-today, cap-derived finish estimate labelled an estimate.
- **Composer + variables UI** with `{{token}}` insertion from contact fields/attrs, tag + explicit-contact audience picker, instance picker, priority, optional schedule.
- **Progress funnel**: `campaign_counters` served from Postgres rollups with `deferred` as a **derived display bucket**, hourly recompute job, `campaign.progress` outbox event at most once per batch, live over SSE.
- **Broadcast list + detail routes** with keyset pagination, Pause/Cancel with explicit confirm (cancel dialog states already-sent messages are not recalled and not refunded).
- **Receipt → funnel stamping** at P21's one call site (carried from P23).

## Risks / gotchas specific to this phase
- **Honest copy.** `BROADCAST_DISCLOSURE` verbatim, always co-present with any surface string containing "Broadcast". The finish time is an **estimate, not a guarantee**. The per-workspace frequency line ships verbatim — it exists so the product never nudges a customer toward hand-rolled number rotation. Never "ban-proof", never "instant bulk sending", never a claim that pacing prevents restrictions. Prices are our pricing, not a provider cost pass-through.
- **`deferred` is a derived display bucket.** Never add it to `broadcast_recipient_status` and never write a row per pacing deny.
- **No faster mode.** A tier-1 account with a 5,000 audience sees ~250 days and two options: reduce the audience, wait for warm-up. Any "priority boost" idea that appears mid-session is out of scope and out of bounds.
- **Counters are aggregates.** The recompute job reconciles `campaign_counters` to `campaign_recipients` row truth; per-send-result increments on the hot counter row are a dead-tuple generator — prefer batch/hourly reconciliation plus the derived read.
- **`completed` is written by the funnel recompute** when no recipient remains non-terminal (P23 step 6 left this to the funnel); `nextCampaignState()` is the only transition table.
- **Receipt counts are a lower bound** (P21: a receipt arriving before `message_wa_ids.message_id` resolves is counted unmatched and lost). Say so in the funnel copy.
