---
title: What Safe Mode does and does not do
description: An honest list of what Safe Mode covers and what it cannot cover, so you know exactly where the responsibility sits.
order: 2
---

Safe Mode paces your sending and watches your account's real signals. It reduces the risk of triggering spam or rate-limit signals from sending too fast or too cold. It cannot prevent or guarantee against WhatsApp restrictions — bans also come from recipient reports, message content and account reputation, which no sender-side pacing can control.

## What Safe Mode does

- Paces your sending, so a number is never pushed faster than its current tier allows.
- Watches the number's real signals continuously, not just at send time.
- Runs a warm-up ramp for new numbers instead of starting them at full volume.
- Stops sending on a number the moment WhatsApp signals a restriction.
- Keeps every queued message when it stops — nothing queued is discarded.
- Tells you what happened, both on the dashboard and through a webhook if you have one configured.

## What Safe Mode does not do

- It does not prevent or guarantee against WhatsApp restrictions.
- It does not resume sending by itself after a restriction — that decision is yours.
- It does not switch you to another number.
- It does not work around a restriction in any way; WP has no such feature and will not build one.
- It does not control recipient reports, your message content, or your account's reputation — those are outside what any sender-side pacing can touch.
