---
title: Groups and why they are capped
description: Why group sending has a much smaller cap than one-to-one sending, and what happens when a group permission problem occurs.
order: 4
---

Sending promotional messages into WhatsApp groups is one of the highest report-rate behaviours on the platform. A single annoyed member can report the message, and group reports are visible to WhatsApp in a way one-to-one messages are not. WP caps group sending, disables it during warm-up, and switches it off first when your account's health signals worsen — but a group blast is riskier than the same message sent one-to-one, and no pacing changes that.

## A much smaller daily cap

Group sending has its own daily cap, and it is much smaller than the cap for one-to-one sending.

## Disabled during warm-up

A newly connected number cannot send to groups at all until it has moved past warm-up.

## The first thing switched off

If your account's health signals worsen, group sending is the first capability WP switches off — before one-to-one sending is affected.

## A permission problem affects only that group

If you are no longer an admin in a group, or the group is set to announce-only, only that group's messages fail. Your whole number is never paused because of a single group's permission state.

## What v1 does not do

WP does not add or remove participants in a group, and does not promote or demote anyone. Leaving a group is always allowed.
