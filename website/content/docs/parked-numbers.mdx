---
title: Parked numbers
description: What parking a number means, what happens to messages while parked, and when a number gets parked.
order: 5
---

Parked — not connected. This number is not receiving messages while parked. Messages people send you during this time may not appear after you reconnect. Queued messages are safe and will send when you reconnect.

WhatsApp's server-side buffer for an offline linked device is neither documented nor unlimited — we don't know how much is retained or for how long.

## Parking frees a connected slot

While a number is parked, it does not count against your connected-number usage.

## Coming back online needs no new QR

Reconnecting a parked number does not require scanning a new QR code or re-linking the device.

## Parking is always a choice

A number is parked only when you, or WP staff acting on an audited request, choose to park it. WP never parks a number automatically in response to a restriction, and never cycles numbers on and off to save resources.

## When there is no free slot

If you try to bring a number online and there is no free connected slot, WP tells you which numbers currently hold the slots. It never silently parks another number to make room.
