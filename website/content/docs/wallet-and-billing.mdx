Sending is prepaid from a wallet, denominated in Indian paise, and charged per message at result time — when WhatsApp accepts the message.

## What is never charged

A message that never reaches "sent" is never charged. That covers a message that failed, one you cancelled, one to a recipient who opted out, one blocked by a content guard, or one that was part of a cancelled campaign.

## What is charged, and why it is not refunded

Once WhatsApp has acknowledged a message, it is charged and not refunded — even if it is later unread, the recipient has blocked you, or the number turns out not to be on WhatsApp. WhatsApp gives no reliable after-acknowledgement undeliverable signal, and we will not pretend otherwise.

## When WP cannot tell what happened

If WP cannot tell whether a message was delivered — for example after a crash or a disconnect mid-send — it does not resend the message, and it does not charge for it until a person reviews what happened.

## Top-ups are manual

Top-ups are made by UPI or bank transfer. You submit the transaction reference (UTR), and a person at WP approves the top-up, same business day at best.

## When the wallet is empty

When the wallet runs out, sending pauses. It resumes by itself once a top-up is approved — this is the one thing that resumes automatically, and it is about money, not about a WhatsApp restriction.
