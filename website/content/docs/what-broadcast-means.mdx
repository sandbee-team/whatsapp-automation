What "Broadcast" means in WP. WhatsApp's own broadcast lists have a limitation most people discover the hard way: a broadcast-list message is only delivered to recipients who have already saved your number in their phone. Everyone else silently receives nothing. WP does not use broadcast lists. When you send a WP Broadcast, we create one individually-addressed message per recipient — the same thing as if you had opened each chat and typed it yourself — and we send them one at a time, paced, from your connected number. That is why a broadcast to 2,000 people takes hours or days rather than seconds: the pacing is the product. It also means each recipient sees a normal one-to-one message from you, can reply to it, and their reply lands in your Inbox. A Broadcast is not a licence to exceed your account's daily cap — it simply takes as long as your cap allows, and the panel shows you the honest estimated finish time before you start.

## The pre-flight estimate

Before you start a broadcast, the pre-flight screen shows an estimated finish time based on your current daily cap. This is an estimate, not a guarantee.

## A broadcast never raises your cap

Starting a broadcast does not change your number's daily cap. It simply uses the cap that is already in place, the same as any other sending.

## Pause and cancel keep what already sent

If you pause or cancel a broadcast partway through, every message already sent stays sent — nothing is undone. The remaining, unsent messages stop.

## Replies land in your Inbox

Because each message is a normal one-to-one message, any reply a recipient sends comes back as an ordinary conversation in your Inbox.
