## Roles

You are the controller of your contacts, message content, and the data your workspace collects. WP is the processor, acting on your instructions to provide the messaging service.

## Categories of data processed

- Contacts
- Message content
- Delivery metadata
- Opt-outs
- Consent attestations

## Security measures

Session credentials are envelope-encrypted with purpose-separated keys held outside the database. Tenant isolation is enforced at the database row level. Backups are encrypted and stored off-site, using a passphrase kept separate from the credential keys. Restore drills are run to confirm those backups are actually recoverable.

## Retention and deletion

Message bodies are purged within 7 days of a deletion request. Audit records and opt-out tombstones are retained beyond that, for compliance and to honour opt-outs correctly.

## Breach notification

We notify you without undue delay, and no later than 72 hours after we confirm a breach affecting your data.

## Carve-outs

Retention beyond your instructions may apply where WP has a legal obligation to keep a record, or a legitimate interest in retaining audit and opt-out records to operate the service safely.
