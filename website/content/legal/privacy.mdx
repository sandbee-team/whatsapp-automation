## Website analytics

The website's analytics collects only an event name and the page path. It never collects a query string, never sets a cookie, and WP does not store your IP address from these events — and it only runs when an analytics endpoint is configured for this deployment.

## The contact form

The contact form stores your name, email, company, phone, message, the page it was submitted from, and any UTM tags present at submission. It also stores an irreversible hash of your IP address, never the address itself, kept for 12 months.

## Your message content

WP is a linked device, so WP's servers process your message content in the clear. WhatsApp's end-to-end encryption protects messages between devices; it does not and cannot hide content from a linked device you authorised.

## Our role

For your messaging data, WP acts as a processor: you are the controller of your contacts and message content, and WP processes it on your behalf to provide the service.

## Retention

| Data                           | Retention                             |
| ------------------------------ | ------------------------------------- |
| Message jobs and send attempts | 13 months                             |
| Delivery events                | 90 days                               |
| Audit logs                     | 24 months minimum, never auto-deleted |
| Login sessions                 | 30 days past expiry                   |
| Webhook delivery records       | 30 days                               |
| Number health samples          | 90 days                               |
| Opt-outs and consent records   | For the life of the workspace         |
| WhatsApp session credentials   | Until you unlink or log out           |

## Export and deletion

Data export and deletion are handled by request to WP support in this release. Self-service export and deletion are planned for a later release.
